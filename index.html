About The Project

A dual-interface system designed for autonomy and safety.

🕶️

Smart Glasses Interface

Equipped with a simulated spatial radar, live camera feeds, and turn-by-turn voice navigation to guide users safely around obstacles and towards their destination.

📍

Caregiver Dashboard

Provides peace of mind with real-time GPS tracking, live video streams, system event logging, and instant audio ping capabilities to assist the user remotely.
